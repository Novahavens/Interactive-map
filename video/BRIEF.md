## Intent

Launch the new Property Map features and teach the four-step flow — Pick, Find, Add, Send — in about 75 seconds. Confident, quick, keynote-lite; entertaining without hiding the education. The plan (script, screens, edit, music) was reviewed and approved in chat on 12 Oct 2026, so there is no separate storyboard review.

## Assets

- assets/clips/{pick,find,add,send,hero}.mp4 — real screen recordings of the app (fictional demo data), 2160x1214, recorded by capture/record.mjs.
- assets/stills/mobile-*.jpg — phone screenshots (light + dark).
- audio/vo/*.wav — Kokoro `af_heart` narration, one file per line.
- audio/music/advertime.mp3 — "Advertime", FreePD, CC0. Start 20s into the track so its lift (track 0:32) lands on the title at 0:12.
- assets/fonts/geist*.woff2 — the app's own Geist / Geist Mono (OFL).

## Customizations

- Word-highlighted captions throughout the narration.
- Step chip "01 / 04" … on every tutorial scene; ⌘K keycap overlay on the Pick step.
- Zoom/punch-ins on the UI region the voiceover is talking about.
- Music ducked/carved under the voiceover.

## Notes

- Demo data only — no real claimant names or addresses on screen.
- Pixabay was blocked (bot challenge); FreePD's CC0 catalogue via the Internet Archive was used instead.
