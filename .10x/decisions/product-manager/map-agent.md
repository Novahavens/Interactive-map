# Product — map-agent (26 Nov 2026)

## Problem
Account managers spend their time searching for homes. Goal: they open a claim and the ranked options are already there.

## Scope (owner's decisions, 26 Nov)
All nine features in one release: outside homes, Best match, agent chat (bubble, bottom right), add outside home, submit status, Mine / All, Nearsite tab, Find phone, text templates. Sign-in follows in the next release.

## Out of scope
Google sign-in, AI calling, automated logins to listing sites.

## Success
- Time from claim received to first option submitted goes down.
- Options submitted per claim goes up.
- Account managers use the chat to adjust a search instead of searching by hand.

## Defaults taken
- Submit status is read-only on the map until sign-in.
- "Pick up" on a Nearsite claim opens it; it does not assign it.
- Find phone ships switched off until the lookup accounts exist.
